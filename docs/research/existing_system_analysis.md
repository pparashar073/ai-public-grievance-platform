# Existing System Analysis

## Introduction

The Government of India provides several grievance redressal mechanisms to enable citizens to register complaints and seek resolutions from public authorities. Among these, the Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System (CPGRAMS) serves as the primary digital platform for grievance management.

Recent research, including the Grahak Nyay framework, has explored the use of Artificial Intelligence to enhance these systems through intelligent complaint processing and officer assistance.

This analysis examines the strengths and limitations of existing systems to identify opportunities for AI integration.

---

## CPGRAMS

### Purpose

CPGRAMS is an online grievance redressal platform developed for citizens to submit complaints against government departments and monitor their resolution.

### Strengths

- Nationwide accessibility
- Online complaint submission
- Complaint tracking
- Department-wise routing
- Transparent grievance status
- Centralized grievance management

### Limitations

- Manual complaint analysis
- Manual department selection
- Long complaint reading time
- Duplicate complaint submissions
- Limited intelligent search
- No AI-assisted complaint drafting
- No complaint summarization
- Limited multilingual intelligence
- Limited decision support for officers

---

## Grahak Nyay Research

The Grahak Nyay framework demonstrates how Large Language Models (LLMs) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) can improve grievance management.

### Key Contributions

- AI-powered complaint assistant
- Structured complaint generation
- Knowledge retrieval using RAG
- Officer assistance
- Better complaint quality
- Improved citizen guidance

### Current Limitations

- Research prototype
- Limited deployment scope
- Dependent on knowledge base quality
- Requires continuous updates
- LLM hallucination risks

---

## Observations

The analysis indicates that current grievance systems successfully digitize complaint submission but still rely heavily on manual processing.

AI can improve:

- Complaint quality
- Complaint classification
- Duplicate detection
- Department recommendation
- Complaint summarization
- Priority prediction
- Officer decision support
- Administrative analytics

These observations form the basis of the proposed AI Public Grievance Platform.
